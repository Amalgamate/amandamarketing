Service detail

Make common questions easier to answer.

A well-scoped chatbot can answer approved frequently asked questions, point people to useful information, and collect the details your team needs to help. It should also make it easy to reach a person when the question needs judgment.

A simple example

What this could look like

A visitor asks about delivery areas and opening hours. The chatbot can share the approved details, then offer a contact route if the visitor has a more specific request.

What we can help with

  • Choose suitable questions and define what the bot should not answer.
  • Prepare and organize approved answers and links.
  • Set up contact capture or handoff to your team.
  • Test common questions, fallback responses, and escalation paths.
